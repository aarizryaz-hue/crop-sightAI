The problem

A new worker sees a strange leaf. Words get in the way.

Most extension materials are written for growers and pest control advisers, who often have degrees and read English fluently. Many farmworkers speak Spanish or Indigenous languages, and some have limited formal schooling.

When a symptom is unfamiliar, describing it in a long English form takes longer than pointing at it. Field Signal lets the worker show the problem and choose a visual label, so the supervisor gets a clear, shareable report.

Designed around the findings

  • Pictures first. Symptom categories are illustrated, so reading is optional.
  • Bilingual by default. Every label appears in English and Spanish, with read-aloud where the browser supports it.
  • Built with the field in mind. Large tap targets, low-literacy wording, and offline-capable core features.
  • Honest about limits. Field Signal does not diagnose plant disease. It documents what was observed for a qualified person to review.
