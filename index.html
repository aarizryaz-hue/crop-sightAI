Why CropSight

A photo can start
a better conversation.

An unfamiliar symptom can be hard to describe. CropSight puts the photo and a short bilingual explanation together, so a worker has something concrete to show a supervisor.

Two stages. One simple flow.

Seeds focuses on early growth. Harvest focuses on ripeness and visible produce damage. Both start with a photo.

Built for the handoff.

English and Spanish appear together. A concise supervisor summary keeps the observation easy to share.
