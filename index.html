Why CropSight

A photo can start
a better conversation.

CropSight helps a farmworker turn an unfamiliar crop symptom into concrete observations to discuss with a supervisor.

Show the evidence.

Each possible cause points back to something visible. A whole-plant view and a close-up give the model different information.

Ask a better next question.

A short field observation can help separate similar-looking problems. Your answer informs a fresh analysis.

How it works & credits

The vision model compares crop photos, links possible problems to visible evidence, and chooses a useful next observation.

CropSight uses a pretrained model through an API; it does not train a new model. Built with assistance from OpenAI Codex and Claude. Research context: UC ANR and Andrea G. Juarez, UC Davis.

Accuracy has not been measured on a labeled crop dataset. Spanish wording has not received a professional review.
