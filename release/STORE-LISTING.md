# CropSight — draft Play listing

App name: **CropSight**

Short description: **Turn crop photos into clear observations for your supervisor.**

Full description:

CropSight helps you describe unfamiliar crop conditions and prepare a clear conversation with your supervisor.

Choose Seeds for early growth or Harvest for visible produce condition. Add a whole-plant photo and, if useful, a close-up of the same plant. AI organizes visible observations, possible explanations and checks that could help distinguish them. A short follow-up question can help refine the observation. Copy the resulting summary to share with your supervisor.

- Compare a whole-plant view with a close-up.
- See numbered model observations and the photos they refer to.
- Keep uncertain crop identities explicitly tentative or unknown.
- Switch between English and Spanish in Settings.
- Report incorrect, unsafe or offensive AI results inside the app.

AI analysis requires internet. The interface can open offline after installation. Photos are sent for analysis only after consent. CropSight does not establish a disease diagnosis, seed viability, food safety or permission to harvest or treat a crop. Confirm observations with your supervisor or agricultural adviser.

Suggested category: Productivity. The publisher must confirm category, target audience, content rating and pricing in Play Console.
